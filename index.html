Welcome to Divya Gyan Niketan

Divya Gyan Niketan is an educational institution located in Deepnagar, Nalanda, Bihar. Our aim is to provide quality education while developing discipline, confidence and moral values among students.

We believe that education is not only about academic achievement but also about developing responsible, knowledgeable and compassionate citizens.

About Our Institute

A Premier Educational Institution in Deepnagar

Divya Gyan Niketan is a distinguished institute located in the historically enriched district of Nalanda, Bihar. Built on the core ideals of ancient knowledge and contemporary education, our school aims to foster holistic growth for every student.

Our campus features a state-of-the-art Three-Storey Building Structure designed to provide a spacious, well-ventilated, and safe environment for learning. Equipped with bright classrooms, modern science laboratories, and interactive learning spaces, each floor is tailored to cater to distinct academic grade levels.

We focus not only on academic excellence but also on discipline, creative expression, and sportsmanship to ensure our students grow into well-rounded individuals.

3-Storey Modern Campus

I welcome parents and guardians to partner with us in this transformative educational journey.

- Dinesh Prasad (M.A. English)

About Our Institute

A Premier Educational Institution in Deepnagar

Divya Gyan Niketan is a distinguished institute located in the historically enriched district of Nalanda, Bihar. Built on the core ideals of ancient knowledge and contemporary education, our school aims to foster holistic growth for every student.

Our campus features a state-of-the-art Three-Storey Building Structure designed to provide a spacious, well-ventilated, and safe environment for learning. Equipped with bright classrooms, modern science laboratories, and interactive learning spaces, each floor is tailored to cater to distinct academic grade levels.

We focus not only on academic excellence but also on discipline, creative expression, and sportsmanship to ensure our students grow into well-rounded individuals.
